Tuning settings

What these values do

P sets correction strength; I corrects persistent error; D adds damping. Set Point uses the sensor’s saved calibration: 1000 is its minimum reading and 2000 its maximum. Receiver influence lets the transmitter shift the set point; 0% leaves the target unchanged.
